Featured Project

01

Vehicle-to-Vehicle Communication Using Li-Fi Technology

A road-safety system where a vehicle's LED headlight sends real-time alerts such as sudden braking, obstacles and emergencies to the vehicle behind it using visible light.

The following vehicle receives the signal through a photodiode and alerts the driver through a display.

Li-Fi Embedded Systems V2V Communication Road Safety
01

02

LED transmitter sends the information through a Li-Fi light signal.

03

04
